Audits

    Run your first audit

    1. Open any public web page in Chrome (your client’s site, a landing page, a product page).
    2. Click the AuditLens icon in the toolbar (pin it via the puzzle icon).
    3. Pick the page type and goal, then press Audit this page.

    You’ll get conversion, SEO and speed findings with clear fixes, a predicted attention heatmap, and a report you can export as PDF.

    What AuditLens accesses
    • Read and change pages you audit: to read the page (headings, buttons, forms, SEO tags), scroll it and take screenshots. Only when you press Audit.
    • Tabs: to open the mobile view and the report.
    • Storage: audits and screenshots stay on this computer.
    • Network: only the page URL goes to Google PageSpeed Insights (if enabled), and only text goes to your AI provider (if you add a key).
